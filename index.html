About Me

I’m a Data and Business Intelligence Analyst with a B.S. in Statistics: Data Science and more than three years of experience across business analysis, data management, operational reporting, and higher-education technology. My work focuses on transforming raw data into accurate, understandable, and useful information that supports better decisions.

My experience spans the full analytical workflow—from understanding a business question and identifying the right data, to cleaning, validating, reconciling, analyzing, and presenting the results. I have maintained 99%+ data accuracy in research environments, analyzed datasets containing more than 500,000 records, developed KPI-driven reporting, and created analytical solutions using SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, Python, and R .

Across my professional experience, I have worked with operational, research, technology, and business data. As a Data Manager at Truman State University, I applied repeatable data-quality processes involving cleaning, validation, reconciliation, and quality control. In university IT, I supported users, maintained technology data, documented recurring issues, and developed a practical understanding of how systems, data, and people interact. In my current Business Analyst role, I analyze occupancy, revenue, booking patterns, and operational performance to support pricing, staffing, resource allocation, and day-to-day decision-making.

I have also expanded my technical foundation through hands-on work with relational databases, data modeling, ETL/ELT, Azure Data Factory, Azure Databricks, PySpark, Apache Spark, and cloud-based data workflows. My projects include business intelligence dashboards, SQL-based exploratory analysis, higher-education survey analytics, large-scale data analysis, and interactive web applications developed with R Shiny.

What I enjoy most is the problem-solving side of analytics: understanding what someone is really trying to learn from the data, building a trustworthy analytical solution, validating the result carefully, and communicating the findings in a way that technical and non-technical stakeholders can use. I’m particularly interested in opportunities where analytics, business intelligence, reporting, data quality, automation, and stakeholder collaboration come together to create meaningful business value.
